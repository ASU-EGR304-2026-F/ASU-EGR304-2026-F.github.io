    Product Requirements

    Project Objective

    This project aims to redefine what it means to be a plant caretaker by adapting existing technologies to provide a streamlined user experience when interacting with plants and improving plant health. The target is to achieve 15% market share after 1 year of sales, driving up the consumer database to include professional gardeners and casual enthusiasts. We believe that simplifying the user interface and having specific focus on the most important factors regarding plant health will entice consumers to have fun with their plants while creating a sense of time efficiency, giving us an edge over existing products. Our product aims to match the amount of data features exhibited by the PLANTSIO Smart Plant Pot while retaining reliability over extended use and differing user preferences. It also aims to improve upon the YAMRON 4-in-1 Soil Moisture Meter by being responsive to individual user preferences by being easily configurable to display exactly what users want.

    Stakeholders

    • Target group Adults aged 25-65 who are nature-focused or want to spice up their environment. They are enthusiastic and optimistic about decoration. The main attraction is having a successful hobby without unnecessary work.
    • Target purchaser TPersons with disposable income looking for a gift for a loved one or hobbyists with free time and a desire for gardening.
    • Customer service Desire product that is intuitive for identifying user error, hardware simple for technological troubleshooting, and consistent among manufacturing for limited variance in user experience.
    • Marketing & Sales division Desire unique and desirable selling points that differentiate the product from the state of the art of the market. Selling points relate to effectiveness of plant interaction, ease of use, and extended features not properly addressed by other products.
    • Retailers Require products that don’t degrade during shipping and storage including product case, sensitive electronics, and adjustable/moving parts. Anticipate product dimensions to be efficient for storage and packaging that are enticing to customers while being safe from theft.

    Use Cases

    User Story #1: Molly

    Molly just moved into a new apartment in Manhattan that fits within her budget and could use some redecorating. She loves the principles of feng shui and would like a plant to give her good health, a good environment, and something nice to look at. Her busy work schedule leaves little time for her to dedicate extensive time to plant care, resulting in the death of previous plants and her misguided thought that she has a black thumb. Plastic plants don’t quite do the trick, and she can’t relieve stress by grooming them when she needs a break from her hectic schedule. Her new BAMF plant monitor allows her to interact with her new Jade plant as often as she wants without having to worry about plant health and sinking unavailable time into fixing issues. She loves the simplicity and well-rounded features that give her much information at a glance, allowing her to learn through verified statistics about her plant health.

    Molly has purchased plant monitors before, but struggled to set them up and derive effective information about important aspects about her plant. When she packed her previous one when moving to her new apartment, it broke in transit and customer service was unable to help her repair it. Her new plant monitor survived the shipping process, and she didn’t need to call customer service to help troubleshoot problems as the guide was easy and intuitive.

    User Story #2: Judith

    Judith has just retired from her highly successful career and was looking for a new hobby when she fell into gardening. She realized that gardening requires attention to detail and careful understanding of maintenance, which she excels at given her previous experience as a lawyer at a top law firm. She also has a competitive side, and takes pride in entering gardening competitions where she wins many awards. However, as she has gotten older, the physical aspects of gardening have taken a toll on her and she desires to return to the simpler times when she had a direct impact on her plants without sacrificing her body.

    She loves the simple installment process for her outdoor garden, and appreciates the adaptability of the screen so she doesn’t have to bend down and crane her neck to view the screen. The user interface is convenient for her and not too technologically advanced while also exhibiting sufficient information about the holistics of her plants. She has even been able to get her husband Carl to show interest in her hobby due to the simplicity of the monitor and the easy-to-understand data collection and analysis. She recommends her retired friends to get into gardening due to her positive experience with the monitor.

    Aspects

    1. Product Design

      • The product will have a display for moisture, temperature, and wetness in the soil that the product will be in.
      • The product will be deep enough to give accurate readings of surrounding soil that a plant's roots may be pulling nutrients from.
      • The product will be built to be durable in the sense of bending, rust, or corrosion.
      • The product will have a sturdy enclosure to prevent any internal systems from moisture, rain, and other unforeseen circumstances.
      • The product’s display will be large enough to be easily understood by any individual in a way that will prevent eye strain.
      • The product will be portable enough to place and replace in multiple places throughout a larger garden.
      • The product will be battery powered.
      • The product will have a power button and reset button, but aside from that will not provide uses other than the ones stated.
      • The product will be mounted through a stake like feature built into the product.

    2. Software/Functionality

      • The product will be able to read moisture, temperature, and wetness of surrounding soil.
      • The product will be able to determine if soil has a safe amount of moisture.
      • The product will make readings consistent and stable.
      • The product will be easily able to be understood.
      • The product will be able to be reset at any time by the user.

    3. Interactivity & User Experience

      • The product will be able to be read at a glance.
      • The product will be straightforward to the information required.
      • The product will represent important information via dials.
      • The product's setup will be made to be simple for any individual who has not used a plant sensor before.
      • The product will be packaged with instructions on how to insert and read the product.
      • The product will be able to have its current information reset by the user at any time.

    4. Customization

      • The product will be sold in two separate colors for different visibility purposes.
      • The product may have different lengths of probes for deeper soils.
      • The product will display units for each dial that is being displayed.
      • The product will have a red, yellow, green area on each dial to display what are considered good, mediocre, and bad conditions.

    5. Manufacturing

      • The product should be fully manufactured and ready to use out of the box.
      • The product should be manufactured with the minimum number of parts possible to deliver the performance expected by the customer.
      • The product will be composed of individual replaceable parts that constitute a whole part for ease of repair or replacement.
      • The product will be manufactured in a way that when it does eventually fail, the product will not destroy or damage its surroundings or the habitat in which it was installed.

    6. Safety

      • The product will have a secure place for batteries and other electrical components assuring they will not take damage from weather or other conditions.
      • The product will be safe to use outdoors without worry for any part of the product.
      • The end of the probe will be dull enough to not puncture any living matter.
      • No electrical component will be placed externally.

    Requirement Criteria Specifications

    • The product will accurately display the proper PH rating of the soil within 5 seconds of touching the soil.
    • The product will display soil inaccuracies such as disproportionate nitrogen levels in the soil within 10 seconds of touching the soil.
    • The product will have a user interface that is easy to read and understand by someone with 20-20 vision from a distance of 5 feet.
    • The product will not be distressed, strain, or destroyed by surviving a free fall from the height of 6 feet.
    • The product will not be able to recycle old storage data once the owner presses the reset button within 10 seconds.
    • The product should take someone with a Mensa IQ score of at least 100 no longer than 15 minutes to install into their home garden.
    • The product should be able to be installed by any human with the physical capacity to lift 5 pounds above their shoulders for longer than 60 seconds.
    • The installation and programming manual should be able to be seen from a distance of 2 feet away by anyone with 20-20 vision.
    • The product will securely hold batteries and the casing that surrounds them should resist weather corrosion and sun decay for a minimum of 20 years.
    • The tip of the soil meter will not be able to penetrate any surface with a fracture toughness of at least 6KJ / m^2 .
    • The Instruction Manual should take someone with a Mensa IQ score of at least 100 no longer than 15 minutes to understand and comprehend fully to begin using the product.

    Open Questions

    • Can we improve our design for battery storage on the product to make it more weather resistant?
    • Can we improve our fastener mechanisms that allow the product to attach to a stable support easier?
    • Can we make the product more durable and less susceptible to natural decay via being exposed to sunlight for extended periods of time?
    • Can we make the product for less money without sacrificing the quality that the customer will expect from us?
    • Can we make the product compatible with our other products so that we can sell them as kits?
