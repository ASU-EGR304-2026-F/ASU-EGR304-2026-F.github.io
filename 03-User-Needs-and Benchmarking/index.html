    1. XLUX Plant Soil Moisture Meter, for Indoor and Outdoor use, Battery-Free

    • Price: $9.99

    • Vendor: Amazon

    • Description: The XLUX soil moisture meter is a reliable, easy to use tool that has been trusted since 2015. Its battery free probe measures moisture directly at the root level, while the large color coded display makes it easy to know when plants need water. The slim single probe design helps protect delicate roots and works well in different types of soil. It also includes a protective sleeve and stainless steel probe for durability.

    Positive Comments
    Voice of the Customer Restated Customer Need
    "I used to think I didn't have a green thumb, but over the years, I've tried and tried and YouTube has helped me learn a ton about caring for house plants. I now have a jungle in all three of my apartment rooms! I still have a mishap on occasion and honestly, it's usually about watering. I got tired of sticking my finger into dirt and started using a chopstick and checking to see if the bottom was moist. Meh. Then I figured I'd give this moisture meter a try. I'm so glad I did. It works really fast (instantly, really) and is so far very accurate. It seems well made and is sturdy. I expected more of a cheaply made item and I'm pleasantly surprised that this seems well made, especially for the low price. No batteries needed, which is so great. You don't need instructions - you just stick the probe into the bottom of your soil and it tells you the moisture content. You need to know the needs of each plant and water or not water based on the reading. The outer box comes with a guide on watering requirements for a large variety of plants, which would very helpful for beginners, but I know what each of my plants needs are, so I tossed the box. The probe is really long, so I'm happy about that feature. The probe is stainless and seems very durable. For $10, this is a must have if you are a plant owner. I wish I hadn't waited so long to pick this up!" 1. The Product is durable (explicit)
    2. The Product is easy to understand (Explicit)
    3. The product is precise (explicit)
    Voice of the Customer Restated Customer Need
    "A very simple, basic product. No electronics, no moving parts. Just stick the metal rod into the plant soil (I usually check in two or three different spots) and it tells you on the dial how dry or moist the soil is. Some plants don't like the soil to ever get dry, other plants should definitely be allowed to dry out before being watered, so you need to know about your particular plant to fully benefit from this product. This moisture meter does prevent overwatering (which is a frequent cause of root rot and plant death). My only negative note is that the meter seems a bit fragile: I dropped one on the floor and although it looked fine, it stopped working (it would always read totally "dry"). I think the probe got disconnected from the meter inside the plastic top as a result of the impact sustained when it hit the floor. That's why I bought a second one." 1. The Product has good ease of use (explicit)
    2. The product needs to be strong (latent)
    3. The product should display lots of information (latent)
